## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Switch to spec** Each boss row on the Loot still to come reminder carries a button for the loot spec its marked items need, setting it there and then. Only specs your character has are offered.

## [1.19.0] - 2026-09-10

### Improved
- **Loot still to come** The reminder in a raid or a dungeon is now a row for each boss, carrying its portrait and the icons of the wishlist items it drops. Hover an icon to see the item.
- **Bonus roll odds on the reminder** Each row gives your chance of a wishlist item and what a loot spec switch would make it. A dungeon is a single row, because a keystone charge is spent on the whole instance.
- **The item that wants the switch** An item your loot spec cannot be given is greyed and struck through on the row, and the line beside it names the spec those items need.
- **The reminder follows your loot spec** Changing loot spec while it is up redraws it, and closes it once the switch leaves nothing to say.
